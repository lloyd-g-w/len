Len is a calm, handwriting-first notes app. Write with a stylus (Samsung S Pen and other active pens), your finger or a mouse, on paper that feels like paper and a canvas that never ends.

<b>Notebooks and canvases</b>
• Paged notebooks with paper templates (ruled, grid, dotted, Cornell, music staff, planners) and covers
• A truly infinite canvas that stays fast however much you draw
• Import PDFs to annotate them, or add their pages to a notebook
• Scan paper with the camera: edges found for you, cleaned up in colour, greyscale or black and white
• Save notebook styles and pick a default for new notebooks
• A minimap on the infinite canvas
• Folders, favourites, recent notes and a trash you can restore from

<b>A pen that keeps up</b>
• Low-latency ink with pressure, palm rejection and a stabiliser
• Pens, highlighter, eraser (or flip the S Pen / hold its button), lasso and shapes
• Scribble over ink to erase it; hold the pen still to snap a shape
• Reshape shapes by their handles and give closed ones a fill
• Lasso as freehand or rectangle, choosing what it selects
• A zoom window: write large in a box, small on the page
• Every edit can be undone

<b>Everything a note needs</b>
• Text boxes, images and audio recordings next to your writing
• Search, page overview, bookmarks and presentation mode with a laser pointer that can stay until you stop writing
• Export to PDF and PNG, or share whole notebooks as Len files

<b>Your notes stay yours</b>
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Works offline; back up the whole library to one file
• Optional sync between your devices through a small server you host yourself

Len also runs on Windows, Linux and in the browser.
