# Owner asks — 2026-10-25

Toolbar, icons, selection, shapes and eraser hover.

## Toolbar (like Notewise)
- Tapping the pen opens a small submenu with the pen's options. Same for the
  other tools that have options (highlighter, eraser, laser, shapes, lasso).
- Make the pen options much simpler: no colours and no nested submenus.
- Keep colours apart from the tool options.
- Set how many quick colours and sizes there are in Settings, not in the
  pen's options.

## Iconography
- The icons look bad; redo them as one consistent set. The selection
  (lasso) icon most of all.

## Selection
- Make the lasso stroke cleaner. Don't tint or highlight the selected items;
  the selection frame is enough.

## Shapes
- Smoother shapes.
- Hold to snap: while the pen is still down after the snap, moving it
  rotates and resizes the shape live.
- Circles are real circles, not wobbly ellipses.
- Better curves: replace the Bézier fitting that looks wrong. Look at
  alternatives (e.g. κ-curves, Euler spiral / clothoid splines,
  centripetal Catmull–Rom).

## Eraser hover
- With the eraser selected, the hover shows the pen's preview. It should
  show only the eraser's.

## Laser
- No dot at the start or end of the laser trail.

## Done (2026-10-25, merged into main)
- Toolbar: tap the selected tool for one flat menu (no colours, sizes or
  submenus); quick colour and size counts in Settings → Toolbar.
- Icons: one drawn family for the tools (new lasso); commands stay
  Material Symbols.
- Selection: thin dashed lasso and frame, no fill, selected items untinted.
- Hover: only the active tool's preview (eraser ring alone).
- Shapes: turn and scale while held after the snap; true circles; outlines on
  the true curve; κ-curves for curves.
- Laser: trail without a dot.
- Toolbar (PR #1, after "the colours still appear on the rest of the bar"):
  the main bar holds the tools only; the active tool's widths | colours | ⚙
  options sit in a sub-bar pill under it (over the phone's row, beside the
  floating palette), as in Notewise; the eraser's sizes | whole/partial.
- Scribble to erase needs 3 passes instead of 4 (there, back, there: a
  "Z"; tighter and erasing at pen-up), on fix/scribble-fewer.
- Rounder icons and toolbar (after "make the icons of the subbar pen sizes
  be rounded … make everything less square"), on feat/round-icons: width
  slots are round-ended lines with an accent dot under the chosen one; the
  tool icons redrawn as a soft family (round caps and joins, rounded
  bodies, a round-dashed lasso with a round knot, a halo for the laser);
  Material Symbols Rounded at weight 400 instead of Sharp 300; radii
  4/8/12/16 (from 2/4/6/8), `md` tool plates, round slot plates, pill
  switches.
- To check on the tablet: hover under the S Pen, drift after a snap,
  the circle threshold (17 %), the curve tool by touch.
- Minimap gestures move the minimap (after "the minimap zooming and panning
  etc to be of the actual minimap, not the canvas"), on fix/minimap-gestures:
  pinch, two-finger drag, trackpad, wheel and Ctrl+wheel zoom and pan the map
  only; tap and drag still move the view; the zoomed map follows the view (an
  edge arrow when panned away from it); a fit button above it (and *Fit
  minimap* in its menu) fits it again; blocks cached as a picture.
