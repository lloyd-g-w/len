# Owner asks — 2026-10-25

Toolbar, icons, selection, shapes and eraser hover.

## Toolbar (like Notewise)
- Tapping the pen opens a small submenu with the pen's options. Same for the
  other tools that have options (highlighter, eraser, laser, shapes, lasso).
- Make the pen options much simpler: no colours and no nested submenus.
- Keep colours apart from the tool options.
- Set how many quick colours and sizes there are in Settings, not in the
  pen's options.

## Iconography
- The icons look bad; redo them as one consistent set. The selection
  (lasso) icon most of all.

## Selection
- Make the lasso stroke cleaner. Don't tint or highlight the selected items;
  the selection frame is enough.

## Shapes
- Smoother shapes.
- Hold to snap: while the pen is still down after the snap, moving it
  rotates and resizes the shape live.
- Circles are real circles, not wobbly ellipses.
- Better curves: replace the Bézier fitting that looks wrong. Look at
  alternatives (e.g. κ-curves, Euler spiral / clothoid splines,
  centripetal Catmull–Rom).

## Eraser hover
- With the eraser selected, the hover shows the pen's preview. It should
  show only the eraser's.
